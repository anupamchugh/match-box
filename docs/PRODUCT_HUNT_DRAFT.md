# Product Hunt Draft — Match Box

## Tagline

Private context for dating conversations — on your Mac, under your control.

## Description

Match Box turns a screen you choose in iPhone Mirroring into reviewable local
context. It keeps visible facts, conversation history, and uncertainty together
so you can decide what to say next without handing your dating life to a cloud
agent.

It captures one visible screen at a time, runs OCR on-device, asks before it
saves anything, and can use Apple’s on-device Foundation Model for an optional
grounded review. It cannot send a message, like, swipe, match, log in to a
dating app, or run in the background.

## Launch assets to create with fictional data

1. Capture and approval flow.
2. Inbox with one fictional conversation.
3. Profile facts separated from unknowns.
4. On-device review that explicitly leaves an abbreviation unresolved.
5. One short demo video: capture, review, save, inspect.

## Do not publish yet

Publication is blocked until the signed app successfully completes a real
owner-selected capture, explicit save, relaunch, and review loop; all public
assets must use fictional data.
